## Response

## DocuSearch ingestion settings

DocuSearch uses:

- **Chunk size:** **900 characters**
- **Chunk overlap:** **140 characters**

These are the default values used across the retrieval configuration. [1]

## Why these values were chosen

The authors state that they tested several chunk sizes before settling on these values:

- **Smaller chunks** reduced **sufficiency scores**, meaning individual chunks were less likely to contain enough information.
- **Larger chunks** reduced **retrieval precision**, meaning retrieval became less focused.

Thus, **900 characters with a 140-character overlap** was selected as a practical balance between providing sufficient context and maintaining precise retrieval. The paper does not provide more detailed experimental results or a separate justification specifically for the 140-character overlap. [1]
